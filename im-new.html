A Personal Invitation

A Word From Our Pastor

It is my great joy to welcome you to New Glory Baptist Church. We are a family of believers here in Ajao Estate, committed to the Word of God, to sincere worship, and to loving one another well.

Whether you're visiting for the first time or looking for a church to call home, I want you to know that you are seen, you are valued, and you are welcome here — just as you are.

I look forward to meeting you in person and hearing your story. Come as you are — we're saving you a seat.

Rev'd Dr. Owolabi Akindayo

What to Expect

We Know Visiting Somewhere New Can Feel Intimidating

Here's a quick guide to help you feel at home.

Worship Style

Our services are Bible-centred and Spirit-filled, with congregational singing, prayer, and a message from Scripture. Expect a warm, welcoming atmosphere rather than a formal or reserved one.

For Kids

Children are always welcome in our services. Call the church office ahead of your visit if you'd like to know more about what's currently available for kids during service.

What to Wear

Come as you are — there's no dress code. You'll see everything from casual to formal, so wear whatever makes you comfortable.

Parking

There's ample car park space within the church premises, so you're welcome to drive in. Call or WhatsApp us on 0806 239 7290 if you need any further guidance ahead of your visit.
