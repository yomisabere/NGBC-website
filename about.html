What We Believe

Our Foundations

As a Baptist church, our convictions are rooted in Scripture and shared with Baptist churches around the world.

  • The Authority of Scripture — the Bible is God's inspired Word and our final authority for faith and life.
  • Salvation by Grace — we are saved through faith in Jesus Christ alone, not by works.
  • Believer's Baptism — baptism by immersion, as a public declaration of faith for those who have personally trusted Christ.
  • The Priesthood of All Believers — every believer has direct access to God and a role to play in His church.
  • The Local Church — a gathered community of believers, called to worship, discipleship, and mission together.
