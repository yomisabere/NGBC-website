Sunday School.

A place to grow richly in reading, studying, understanding, and applying the Word of God — together.

Our Objective

Rooted in the Word, Together

Sunday School exists to give every member of New Glory Baptist Church a sustainable, ongoing rhythm of engaging Scripture — not just hearing it preached, but reading, studying, understanding, applying, and sharing it in community.

We meet every Sunday at 9:00 AM, ahead of the main Worship Service, in classes grouped by age and stage of life. Whether you're new to the Bible or have studied it for years, there's a class for you.
