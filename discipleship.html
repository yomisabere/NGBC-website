Why Join?

The Master's Mandate

Our Discipleship Programme is shaped by the Master's mandate: "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations" (Matthew 28:19).

Whether you're a new believer or have walked with Christ for years, these courses are designed to deepen your relationship with God and equip you for service.
